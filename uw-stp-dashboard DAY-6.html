Key metric definitions

STP Approval Rate
Submissions auto-decisioned end-to-end ÷ total submissions. Card subtext shows the absolute count of STP-approved submissions in scope.
SLA Compliance Rate
Share of submissions decided within the 3.0-day Time-to-Decision SLA. Sub-line shows the count of breaches of the tighter 2.0-day target (coloured by whether breaches rose vs prior).
Referral Rate
Submissions routed to a human underwriter ÷ total submissions.
Time-to-Decision
Mean days from submission receipt to bind/refer/decline.
Submission Volume
Count of new-business submissions in the selected period.
Manual Review Queue
Open referrals currently awaiting an underwriter (snapshot).
Risk Leakage Count
Bad risks auto-approved by STP today, with aggregate at-risk premium. Detailed on the Risk Leakage tab.
Premium Processed via STP
Gross written premium auto-bound without a human, £m, RLS- and period-scaled.
Loss Ratio
Incurred losses ÷ earned premium (GWP-weighted). Shown on What-If, Insights, Distribution and the Overview Actual-vs-Expected cards.

What-If Simulation — Parameter Reference

  • STP Premium Limit — the max premium a submission can carry and still auto-decision. Controls how large a risk the engine will pass without a human. Raising it → more submissions qualify → STP Rate ↑, but average risk size ↑ → Loss Ratio may ↑ and additional GWP ↑.
  • Max Prior Loss Ratio — ceiling on the applicant's historical loss ratio for STP eligibility. Raising it → admits higher-loss accounts → STP Rate ↑ but Loss Ratio ↑.
  • Risk-Score Ceiling — highest composite risk score still eligible for STP. Raising it → more marginal risks auto-pass → STP Rate ↑ with some Loss Ratio ↑.
